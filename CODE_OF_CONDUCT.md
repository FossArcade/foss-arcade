# Foss Arcade Code of Conduct

**Status:** living community policy — people behavior, not game content  
**Canonical:** this file at repo root (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)  
**Spirit:** FOSS — keep spaces usable for contributing and playing together. Sunshine over secret sauce. No private kingmaking.

Inspired by the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but kept **project-specific and short**—not a giant paste.

---

## Purpose

Keep Foss Arcade spaces usable for **contributing and playing together**: propose, review, fund, race, merge, and discuss without harassment turning the commons into a war zone. Disagree hard on ideas; do not attack people.

---

## Scope

This CoC covers **official Foss Arcade community surfaces**, including:

| Surface | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **GitHub (or forge) org** | Issues, PRs, discussions, project boards under `fossarcade` |
| **Official Reddit** | [r/FOSSArcade](https://www.reddit.com/r/FOSSArcade) |
| **Official Discord** | When / if an official server exists |
| **Shelf comments** | When / if Arcade Shelf hosts moderated comments |

It does **not** police the entire internet, every fork elsewhere, or private chats outside official venues. Forks and mirrors may set their own house rules.

---

## Expected behavior

- **Be respectful.** Critique work and process; do not demean people.
- **Assume good faith** until public evidence says otherwise.
- **Disagree on ideas publicly**—ADRs, issues, scorecards—not via whisper campaigns.
- **No harassment, doxxing, threats, or hate** directed at people.
- **No sealioning forever**—endless bad-faith “just asking” that derails after good-faith answers have been given. Ask once, cite sources, move on or open a focused issue.

Maintainers and stewards are bound by the same rules.

---

## CoC ≠ content policy

| Policy | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **This CoC** | How people treat each other in official spaces |
| **[content-policy.md](./docs/governance/content-policy.md)** | What **game/content** official surfaces will incubate, Shelf-list, or merge |

Sexual or pornographic **content** on official org / Shelf / incubation / `games/` surfaces is a **content-policy** matter. Personal harassment, threats, doxxing, and hate aimed at people are a **CoC** matter. Illegal content remains a hard ban under content policy and applicable law—CoC does not soften that.

---

## Reporting

- Prefer a **public issue** (or `[meta]` thread) when safe and proportionate—sunshine helps the commons learn.
- For sensitive cases, use the process in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) (private advisory when available). Policy shape: [06-security-supply-chain.md](./docs/governance/06-security-supply-chain.md).
- **Anonymous mob pile-ons are not process.** Brigading, dogpiles, and “cancel first, evidence never” are not how Foss Arcade enforces anything.

---

## Enforcement ladder

| Step | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Warning** | Clear public (or private-then-logged) notice of the breach and expected change |
| **2. Temporary ban** | Time-boxed removal from one or more official spaces |
| **3. Permanent ban** | Sustained abuse, severe harm, or repeated temp-ban failures |

Rules:

- Publish a **short public summary** of serious actions **without drama doxxing** (no dumping private details for spectacle).
- **Serious actions** (temp ban of meaningful length, permanent ban, maintainer removal for conduct) require **multi-maintainer confirm** and follow dispute / succession norms in [09-dispute-capture.md](./docs/governance/09-dispute-capture.md)—no solo ban crown.
- **Enforcement reasons are logged** (issue comment, moderation log, or incident note). No silent ghosting as the only record for CoC actions that change someone’s access.
- **Appeals** go through the public process in [09-dispute-capture.md](./docs/governance/09-dispute-capture.md) (maintainer conduct / capture layer and related `[meta]` paths)—not a private Discord veto theater.

---

## No private kingmaking

- Enforcement that cannot be summarized publicly (even at a high level) is suspect.
- Private chat may coordinate triage; it is **not** the authoritative judgment.
- Quiet-root / org power does not excuse CoC breaches—see [09](./docs/governance/09-dispute-capture.md).

---

## Applies to maintainers too

CODEOWNERS, DESIGNERS, org stewards, bots operators, and Shelf moderators are under this CoC. Abuse of block rights or moderation tools for personal vendettas is both a CoC and a [09 dispute / capture](./docs/governance/09-dispute-capture.md) issue.

---

## Not a substitute for law

This CoC is community house rules for official spaces. It is **not** legal advice and does not replace applicable law. Reports of **illegal activity** should go to appropriate platforms and authorities; Foss Arcade may cooperate with lawful process and still apply venue bans. See also [content-policy.md](./docs/governance/content-policy.md) (illegal hard ban) and [07-legal-ip.md](./docs/governance/07-legal-ip.md).

---

## Patching

CoC changes are **`[meta]` slow lane**: PR, rationale, impact on open moderation cases, merge. Mid-drama silent rewrites to favor one camp are themselves capture—treat them that way.
